Farm Empire: From Zero – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Farm Empire: From Zero is a single-player game for iPhone. It works entirely on your device. We do not collect, send or share any information about you.

What the game stores, and where

To let you continue playing, the game keeps the following on your iPhone only:

  • your saved farm (cash, buildings, fields, progress),
  • your settings (volume, graphics, reduced motion, haptics),
  • the pictures in your in-game farm photo album, which the game draws itself from the game world,
  • a small local diagnostics note (how many sessions ended unexpectedly, the last error message, peak memory use), shown to you in Settings.

None of this leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app, and "Start over" in Settings resets your farm.

What the game does not do

  • It makes no network requests. It works without an internet connection.
  • It has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics software.
  • It does not track you, and it does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It has no in-app purchases.
  • It contains no third-party software that collects data. The fonts are bundled with the app.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
