Common questions

Do I need an internet connection or an account?
No. The game is fully offline and has no accounts.

Where is my farm saved?
On your iPhone, and saved automatically about a second after every change and when you leave the game. Nothing is sent anywhere, so deleting the app deletes the farm.

What happens while the game is closed?
Crops grow, workshops finish and trucks arrive. Nothing is lost or punished while you are away. If you have hired workers, they do one round of work when you come back and cost nothing while the game is closed.

How do I start over?
Settings (the gear icon), then "Start over". Your settings are kept.

The game feels slow on my phone.
In Settings, turn off "Sharp graphics" and turn on "Reduce motion".

How do I play?
Settings, then "How to play". The goals at the top of the screen guide you through the first hours.
